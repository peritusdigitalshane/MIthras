## Purpose
This procedure curates the monthly customer report drafted automatically on the first day of each month. The reseller adds commentary that reflects context the platform cannot infer, then releases the report to the customer. Released reports are the primary monthly artefact customers retain for governance and renewal review.

## Audience and authority
Reseller staff whose user record carries a `partner` role on the partner organisation, with scope set to the customer organisation that owns the report. Curation is gated by the `customer_reports.status` column being `draft`. Reports left in `draft` are auto-released five business days after creation; reseller commentary added after auto-release becomes an appended addendum, not a primary edit.

## Prerequisites
- The reporting month has closed and the draft report is visible at `/reports` for the relevant customer.
- You have access to any out-of-platform context required for commentary, including ticketing notes, on-site visit logs, and after-hours escalation records.
- The customer's notification recipients are configured. The release notification dispatches to the addresses recorded for the customer's `incident` and `report` categories.
- You have read the autogenerated content end to end before adding commentary.

## Procedure

1. Open `/reports` in the Mithras console. Reports are listed across every customer in your scope; the current reporting month sorts to the top.
2. Select the row in status `draft` for the target customer to open the report editor.
3. Review the autogenerated sections:
   - `Endpoint inventory and uptime`
   - `Threats blocked, by severity`
   - `Active and resolved incidents`
   - `Defender posture summary`
   - `End-of-life Windows hardening status`, where the customer is on tier `eol_protected`
   - `Vulnerability open and closed deltas`
   - `M365 Identity Threat Detection and Response signals`, where the customer has connected their tenant
   - `AI Triage Agent and Commander autonomous actions`
   - `Subscription notes`
4. Add reseller commentary in the editable blocks:
   - `Executive summary` — two sentences in plain language for the customer's executive audience.
   - `Reseller actions taken this month` — manual and out-of-platform work, including on-site visits and after-hours escalations.
   - `Recommendations for next month` — proposed work, configuration changes, or commercial decisions for customer approval.
5. Select `Preview` to render the Portable Document Format (PDF) preview and confirm formatting.
6. Where the report is ready, select `Release`. The customer receives an email with the PDF attached and a link to their reports archive. Where further edits are required, select `Save draft`. The auto-release timer pauses while unsaved edits exist.

## Verification
- The report row at `/reports` transitions from `draft` to `released` with the release timestamp and the releasing reseller recorded.
- The customer's `customer_reports` archive surfaces the new row, accessible to their administrators.
- A dispatch confirmation is recorded against `public.activity_logs` with `action_type = 'report_email_sent'` for each notification recipient.
- The PDF rendered in `Preview` matches the email attachment received by the customer; differences indicate a stale browser preview and require a fresh render.

## Troubleshooting
- **PDF preview fails to render.** Markdown formatting in the commentary blocks contains a syntax error, commonly an unbalanced table row. Remove commentary blocks until preview succeeds, then reintroduce them individually to isolate the failing block.
- **The customer reports the release email was not received.** Inspect `public.activity_logs` filtered by the customer organisation and `action_type = 'report_email_sent'`. A `failed` outcome captures the underlying Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) error. Escalate persistent SMTP failures to the Mithras SOC.
- **Report figures appear lower than current live counts.** The report locks to a snapshot taken at draft creation. Live views at `/endpoints` and `/incidents` evolve after the snapshot; the report intentionally reflects the close of the reporting month.
- **The report was auto-released before review completed.** Add reseller commentary as an addendum through `Append addendum` in the report editor. Addenda are dispatched to the same recipients and recorded as a distinct `activity_logs` row.

## Audit and compliance
- A row is written to `public.customer_reports` capturing `status`, `released_at`, `released_by`, and the snapshot identifier.
- An entry is written to `public.activity_logs` with `action_type = 'report_released'` recording the reseller, the customer organisation, and the report identifier.
- Each notification dispatch writes a row to `public.activity_logs` with `action_type = 'report_email_sent'` and the recipient address.
- Released reports and their underlying snapshot data are retained for seven years to support customer governance and end-of-financial-year audit.
